About Us

Yard cleanup shouldn't come with fine print.

Pet Waste Wagon is a Charlotte-based dog waste removal service built around three ideas: show the price up front, never lock anyone into a contract, and prove every visit actually happened. That's the whole pitch — no gimmicks, no upsells, just a clean yard on a schedule you can count on.

We love our CLT community, and we love being around animals — Pet Waste Wagon is our chance to do both at once. Every route is a chance to serve the community and help a neighbor out.

What We Stand For

The four things we won't compromise on

Every decision about how we run routes, price plans, and talk to customers gets checked against these.

Transparent pricing

Every rate is posted right on our site. No quote calls, no surprise fees — you see the price before you commit to anything.

No contracts

Weekly or bi-weekly service is a subscription you can cancel anytime. We'd rather earn your business every visit than lock you into fine print.

Proof, not promises

A text and a photo of your closed gate after every visit, so you're never left wondering whether we actually came.

Local and hands-on

Charlotte-owned and Charlotte-run, building our routes one yard at a time — not a call center reading from a script.

Our Promise

If it's not right, we'll make it right.

Trust is earned one visit at a time, so here's exactly what you can expect from every single one.

  • Not happy with a cleanup? Tell us within 24 hours and we'll re-clean it at no charge.
  • We run routes rain or shine. Severe weather gets you a reschedule, never a skipped visit you're still charged for.
  • Waste is double-bagged and hauled off your property — nothing gets left behind.
  • Our tools get cleaned between yards, so nothing travels from one property to the next.
