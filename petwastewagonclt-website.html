Why It Matters

More than an eyesore

Dog waste left in the yard isn't just unpleasant to step around — it's a real health and lawn problem. Here's what a consistently clean yard actually protects.

Stops the spread

Waste carries parasites and bacteria — roundworm, giardia, E. coli — that linger in soil and put pets and kids at risk.

Protects the water

Rain washes uncollected waste into storm drains and Charlotte's creeks. Regular pickup keeps it out of the watershed.

Saves your lawn

Waste left sitting burns and yellows grass. A regularly cleaned yard stays greener with less patchy die-off.

Kills the smell

No more holding your breath by the back door or apologizing to guests — just a yard you actually want to use.

Questions

Before you book

The things people ask us most. Anything else, just call or send a note — we answer same day.

Do I need to be home when you come?

Nope. Most of our customers aren't. We just need access to the yard — an unlocked gate or a gate code you give us at signup. You'll get a text and a photo of the closed gate when we're done, so you know exactly when we came and went.

What if my gate is locked or my dog is outside?

Leave us the gate code in the signup notes and we'll handle it. If your dog is friendly and out in the yard, that's usually fine — just tell us in advance. If your dog isn't comfortable with strangers, let us know and we'll work out a schedule where they're inside during our visit.

What happens in bad weather?

We run our routes rain or shine. If there's severe weather — heavy storms, ice, anything unsafe — we'll push your visit to the next available day and text you about it.

What do you do with the waste?

It's double-bagged and hauled off your property — nothing gets left behind in your bins unless you specifically ask us to. Our tools get cleaned between yards so nothing travels from one property to the next.

Am I locked into a contract?

No contracts, ever. Weekly and bi-weekly plans are subscriptions you can cancel anytime from your account. Switch between plans, pause for a vacation, or cancel outright — no cancellation fee, no phone call required.

I have more than four dogs. Can you still help?

Absolutely. Our posted pricing covers up to four dogs; beyond that (or for unusually large properties) we'll put together a custom quote. Use the quote form below and tell us how many dogs and roughly how big the yard is.

What if I'm not happy with a cleanup?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean it at no charge. We'd rather fix it than have you wondering whether it was worth it.
